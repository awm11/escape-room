PHYSICS ESCAPE ROOMTEMPORA: teacher guide

Groups are stranded on a freezing ship orbiting Mars and must solve puzzles to restart the engines, guided by Mars Base Alpha on the ship's terminal. The terminal is a web page. You need a laptop with a browser and no login.

Prepare before the lesson

Equipment to gather

Per group, as well as the printed resources and a laptop:

  • Pendulum bob on a metre-long string with a loop
  • Stop clock, tape, Blu Tack
  • Colour filters, including red
  • Compass, magnet and magnifying glass (decoys)
  • Clamp stand and clamp
  • An empty, dry kettle
  • 8 zip ties
  • Two padlocks with settable codes, a box for the Orbital Utility Kit, and a lockable cupboard

Each group's kit

Also your pack-away checklist.

  • Do Now for each student
  • Orbital Utility Kit (box)
    • Padlock set to 783
    • Pendulum bob on a pre-measured, knotted string
    • Stop clock and tape
    • Envelope with the puzzle and puzzle hint
    • Coloured filters, including red
    • Decoys: compass, magnet, magnifying glass
  • Kettle, with the torn-out page inside
  • Locked cupboard (Captain's Locker)
    • "Captain's Locker" sign on the front
    • Filter puzzle (black and white grid) inside
    • Padlock set to 478
  • Tempora User Manual, with the "shopping list" puzzle (answer 783)
  • Clamp stand
  • Captain's Code-le stuck under the desk (answer 478)
  • Laptop with the game open

Setting up

  1. On each laptop, open alexmoorephysics.com/escape-room. Full screen looks best.
  2. Leave it on the start screen. Groups can type their crew names before pressing Start mission; the names appear on the mission report.
  3. Stick the Captain's Code-le under the desk.
  4. Lock the black and white filter grid in the cupboard, with the Captain's Locker sign on the front.
  5. Put the manual, with the shopping list inside, on the desk.
  6. Optional: atmospheric music. Option 1 (recommended), option 2, option 3.
Students should not write on any of the sheets. If they need to, they can use a blank piece of paper.

Running the room about 40 minutes

Mars Base Alpha tells students they have about 40 minutes. The cabin temperature in the top corner of the screen drops the whole time and frost creeps in from the edges, but nothing happens when it gets very cold. It's for atmosphere.

  1. Do Now

    Students complete the Do Now; the first letters of the answers spell TEMPORA. Hand over the envelope with the story letter and let them press Start mission.

  2. Repair the heating

    Students pick items from the ship's menu with the arrow keys and Enter, or by tapping. Heating shows 5°C and points them to Call Martian Citadel, where Mars Base Alpha explains the problem.

    Scenes 1–2

  3. Open the Orbital Utility Kit

    Students find the "shopping list" in the ship's manual and solve it to get the 3-digit code 783, which opens the box.

  4. Solve the puzzle to find the kettle

    They fill in the puzzle grid from the envelope to make an image, then go two down and three across to find the kettle on the reverse. The torn-out page inside has a phrase to type in:

    Time's tide waits for no one: set course for the future.

    It must be exact: capitals, spelling and punctuation all count. Quotation marks around it are fine, as are the curly quotes iPads add.

    Scene 3

  5. Test the gravity

    Alpha asks whether gravity feels normal (any answer is fine), then for a measurement. Students follow the pendulum instructions in the manual: time 10 swings and divide by 10. The period is about 2; anything from 1.9 to 2.1 is accepted. They type the number only. An answer near 1 gets a hint to double it.

    Scenes 4–5

  6. Open the Captain's Locker

    Alpha says the access code is under the captain's desk. Students solve the Captain's Code-le to get 478 and open the cupboard to find the black and white grid.

    Scene 6

  7. Restart the engines

    Looking at the grid through the red filter (there's a hint on the bottom of the box) reveals the restart code. In the ship's menu they choose Engines, press Enter, and type 8643.

    Scenes 7–8

  8. Mission report

    The engines start, the cabin warms and Alpha calls to congratulate them. A report then shows the crew names, time taken, wrong answers and coldest temperature. It isn't saved anywhere, so take a photo or press Copy report.

If something goes wrong

  • There's no time limit. The game never times out, so groups can take as long as they need.
  • Page refreshed or closed: reopen it and click Resume from step N on the start screen. The group restarts at the beginning of the scene they were on.
  • Stuck, or skipping ahead: on the start screen, click sys admin (bottom right) and choose a scene. Starting later begins colder, and the mission report notes it wasn't a full run.
  • Waiting for text to type out: pressing Enter finishes the current message instantly.

Afterwards: put everything back where it came from, using each group's kit list as a checklist, and lock the padlocks again ready for the next class.
